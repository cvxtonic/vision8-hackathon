# Vision 8 – Pitch deck

🇬🇧 English · 🇩🇪 [Deutsch](README.de.md)

Three-minute pitch, followed by a two-minute live demo.

| # | Slide | Speaker notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Vision 8** – *The coach who won’t let you off the hook.* | Hook first, no greeting. |
| 2 | **The problem** – *Everyone has motivation. For three days.* | Goals are vague, have no why, and nobody follows up. A coach costs about 150 € an hour, so almost nobody has one. |
| 3 | **The solution** – *Talk. Commit. Follow through.* 01 Talk: 10 minutes with Clara · 02 Goal contract: SMART, 10 years to today · 03 Every day: list, score, evening call | Clara is an AI coach in real-time voice. The talk produces a vision and SMART goals. |
| 4 | **Every day** – *What matters most counts most.* Write one paragraph of the application (top priority, +10) · Phone-free time with Lina (+7) · Go for a run (+6) | Every morning Clara rates the to-dos against the vision and the monthly goal. The most important one is marked in red and earns the most points. |
| 5 | **Live** – *Meet Clara.* | Demo, see below. |
| 6 | **Vision 8** – *Other apps remind you. Clara calls.* | Last line, pause, thank you. |

## Demo (2 minutes)

| Seconds | Screen |
